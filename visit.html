Getting here

Parking, transit and the walk.

Parking

Metered street parking runs along East Georgia and the surrounding blocks. The Chinatown Plaza parkade at 180 Keefer Street is a short walk from the door.

Transit

Stadium Chinatown Station is about a ten minute walk. Several bus routes stop on Main Street, one block from the restaurant.

A note on the area

Chinatown borders the Downtown Eastside and some blocks feel rough around the edges. The street itself is busy through lunch and dinner. As anywhere downtown, keep valuables out of sight in a parked car.

The neighbourhood

One of the oldest Chinatowns in North America.

Since the 1880s

Vancouver's Chinatown took shape in the 1880s, settled by Chinese workers who stayed on after the railway was finished. It grew into one of the oldest and largest Chinatowns on the continent.

A historic site

The neighbourhood is a National Historic Site of Canada, recognized for its storefronts, its family associations, and more than a century of continuous community.

East Georgia Street

Our block sits on the quieter eastern edge of Chinatown, a street of small storefronts and family businesses. The Huynh family opened Phnom Penh here in 1985, and the room has not moved since.

While you are here

Make an afternoon of it.

Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Garden

The first full scale classical Chinese garden built outside China, a short walk west on Carrall Street.

Chinatown Storytelling Centre

Exhibits on the families who built the neighbourhood, on East Pender Street a few blocks from the restaurant.

The surrounding blocks

Bakeries, barbecue shops, coffee and small bars line East Georgia and Main Street. The Millennium Gate stands over West Pender, about ten minutes away on foot.

Come hungry, put your name down, and let the neighbourhood fill the wait.
